About Me

Hello! I am pursuing my Ph.D. at the Felix Bloch Institute for Solid State Physics (Faculty of Physics and Earth System Sciences), Universität Leipzig, starting September 2024.

Prior to moving to Germany, I completed my Integrated B.S.-M.S. in Physics with a minor in Computational and Data Sciences from the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Kolkata (GPA: 8.29/10).

My core academic pursuits revolve around Quantum Sensing and Metrology, nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centers in diamond, quantum optics, and advanced experimental physics instrumentation.
